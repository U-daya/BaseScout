Mission 01 · Crew base scout

Meet the
scout.

A six-wheel rover that drives to candidate base sites, measures the slope and tests the soil before anyone lands.

Mobility

Rocker-bogie suspension.

Six independently driven wheels keep contact with uneven ground, so the rover can climb rocks without tipping.

Power and thermal

Built to survive the cold.

A solar deck on top, a radioisotope power unit at the rear, and gold thermal blankets around the electronics.

Navigation

Eyes two metres up.

Stereo cameras on the mast map the ground ahead and pick a safe route to each pad.

Systems

Every part has a job.

Body, mobility, power, navigation, sampling. In the demo, a real Raspberry Pi joystick drives it and live sensors report back.
